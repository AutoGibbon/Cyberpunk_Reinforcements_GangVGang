fight crash. `Reinforcements_GangVsGang` doesn't hook player spawn at
all — every entry point in this codebase (`ReinforcementsChecksCall`,

among the loaded mods: ArchiveXL, TweakXL, Codeware, or a vanilla resolve
path — no basis to pick one from what's captured here).
